How to correct a section

Tap a section below, then tap Add waypoint to selected section and tap the map on the actual road. A marker will appear. Drag markers to adjust. Recalculate just that section. Remove a mistaken pin with its popup. Corrections save automatically on this device. You can export the project JSON to keep them for another device, and export GPX only if all sections have loaded.

The short coloured sections may still be wrong until individually reviewed. "Routed" does not mean verified. The route line is road-network geometry from the routing service; the dashed line is merely an indication of checkpoints. © OpenStreetMap contributors.
